Repairing is not restoring things to their original state.  
It's not something that only looks backwards.

Repair is creative.

Things can be repaired.

Not only objects or materials.  
The effects of climate change, political/ideological polarization, the
effects of the worst crime ever committed against humanity - slavery.

They can be repaired. Which is not the same as saying that we can erase
the past.

It's not about being fixated in restoring the past. Instead, it’s
celebrating the idea of repair being creative and inclusive. That is, a
way of looking forward and acting on the concrete conditions that we
have at hand.

And imagining futures beyond repair.
